Melancholic Pet
A cute desktop pet companion built with Lua and Rainmeter.
**Play it here:** https://dazaiworks.itch.io/melancholic-pet

1. Insall Rainmeter
    if you don't have it, download and install it from:
    https://www.rainmeter.net/
2. Install the Skin
    - Unzip the folder
    - Move entire "DazaiPet" folder into your Reainmeter Skins folder
        - Usually located at: C:\Users\[YourName]\Documents\Rainmeter\Skins\
3. Load it up!
    - Open Rainmeter (click the raindrop icon in your system tray)
    - Click "Refresh all" in the bottom left
    - Find "DazaiPet" in the list, expand it, and click the Dazai.ini file
    - Ensure Draggable, Save Position, and Snap to edges are checked and Click Through and Keep on Screen are unchecked. Also, ensure that Position is set as "Stay Topmost."
    - Click "Load" on the right side


Controls:
    - Click him to interact
    - Drag him to move him around
    - Right-click to unload or edit settings
    - Double click him to send him to "game mode" so he stays out of your way (double click again to exit)
